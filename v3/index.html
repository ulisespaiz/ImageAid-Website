The gap
The standard screen has a blind spot.
The ankle-brachial index is fast and cheap. For the patients who need it most, it’s also the least reliable.

      

        
PAD
ABI can miss high-risk patients
In diabetes and chronic kidney disease, ABI alone can return false negatives and delay the next test.

        

          

          

          
Non-diagnostic> 1.40
Calcified arteries break the cuff
Medial calcification makes vessels noncompressible. The ankle reads high and the ratio stops meaning much.

        

        
Late
Late detection raises the stakes
Found later, vascular disease is associated with worse outcomes and heavier treatment.

      

      
Sources: CDC, About PAD; Diabetol Metab Syndr 2024.

    

  


  

    

      
Our approach
Pressure is a number. Flow has a shape.
As disease narrows an artery, the Doppler waveform downstream changes in a recognizable way, and calcification doesn’t distort it the way it distorts cuff pressure. That’s the signal ImageAiD is built around.

      

        
Triphasic
Typical normal
Sharp upstroke, brief reversal, small late forward wave.

        
Biphasic
Changing
The late forward wave drops out.

        
Monophasic
Dampened
Slow rise, rounded peak, no reversal.
