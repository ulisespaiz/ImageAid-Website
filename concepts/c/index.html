Why it matters

The cuff can’t read a stiff artery.

6.5M+

Living with PAD

U.S. adults aged 40 and older, per the CDC. Many don’t know it.

>1.40

Off the scale

Calcified arteries resist the cuff. The ankle-brachial index reads high and can’t rule disease out.

Late

Costs more

Found later, vascular disease is associated with worse outcomes and heavier treatment.

The signal

Disease changes the shape of flow. We’re building to read it.

Triphasic

Typical normal

Sharp upstroke, a brief reversal, then a small forward wave.

Biphasic

Changing

The late forward wave drops out. The reversal remains.

Monophasic

Dampened

Slow rise, rounded peak, no reversal. Calcification doesn’t mask it.
