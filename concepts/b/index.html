M—01The gap

The cuff misreads stiff arteries

The ankle-brachial index compares two blood pressures. In patients with diabetes or kidney disease, calcified arteries resist compression, the ankle reads falsely high, and the ratio can land in “normal” or off the scale entirely.

M—02The signal

Shape changes before numbers do

A · Typical normal

Triphasic

Sharp upstroke, a brief reversal, then a small forward wave.

B · Changing

Biphasic

The late forward wave drops out. Reversal remains.

C · Dampened

Monophasic

Slow rise, rounded peak, no reversal. Often seen beyond a significant obstruction, and calcification doesn’t mask it.
